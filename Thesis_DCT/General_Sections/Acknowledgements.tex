\textit{To my family.}

\vspace{0.5cm}

It is often said that a person has two families: the one they are born into and the one they choose. Looking back at the years spent on this doctoral thesis, I feel profoundly fortunate to have been supported by both.

Regarding my chosen family in the academic world, I would first like to express my gratitude to the members of the Granja-Montenegro experimental group. However, my greatest joy has undoubtedly been the computational group. I am immensely grateful for Fabián's vast knowledge, Fonso's directness and ability to get straight to the point, and Xandre's unique philosophical interpretations. I also thank Paula for the calm she brings, Marta for her constant motivation, Anxo for his tranquility and endless anecdotes, and David for his infectious joy and expertise. A very special mention goes to Martin and Pablo for their guidance and instruction during my early steps in this journey. And, of course, a breath of fresh air: Dani and Uxía.

Of course, I must mention my right hand throughout this thesis, Alejandro Seco. From our ideas and discussions to our shared projects, and even living together during a research stay, we have formed a duo better than Mortadelo and Filemón! I am also deeply indebted to my supervisors, Ángel and Rebeca. Thank you for your trust, for your help in searching for a postdoc (even when it wasn't your responsibility), and for your advice beyond the academic realm. You have been true mentors to me (though perhaps having a postdoc in the group with six of us doctoral students might have also been of help!).

Beyond academia, I cannot forget my great friends Ángel and Patri, who, despite not fully understanding what I was doing, were always there to listen. To Lara and Darío, thank you for sharing the hardships of the PhD and for the fun times. And how could I forget the Escape Rooms, basketball games, and long nights spent playing Smite, DBD, or Baldur's Gate with Samuel, Xoel, Berto, Martin, and Marcos. A friendship that began in school and continues to this day is a rare gift, and I couldn't be happier to have you all by my side.

As for the family I was born into, this thesis would not have been possible without the unwavering support of my parents, Maria del Carmen and José. Even without fully grasping the details of my research, they never stopped asking and learning about how the academic system works (my mother even started watching \textit{The Big Bang Theory}!) To my grandmother, Lola, and Vicente, who always hoped I would follow in Jorge Mira's footsteps and become a professor. And to my siblings, Noelia, Julio, and Cris (and my nephews Laura, Sara, Mikel, and Alex), if I had a wonderful childhood, it was thanks to you. Whether I was playing the role of the younger brother or the older one, the football matches in Montalvo, playing in Dorrón with Adrián, and the Wii MarioKart races are memories I cherish. Though life changes, I am sure that even better experiences are yet to come.

I also wish to thank Beatriz Roca, Manuel, and Chemita Carballedo for welcoming me as one of their own for nearly a decade.

Finally, I must thank my life partner, Coral. Thank you for being there every step of the way, through the good and the bad. Your support, trust, and patience have been my anchor. Life is complicated, and we still face moments of being further apart than we would like, but I am certain we will overcome them. We have survived a PIR, a thesis, master's degrees, and all my doubts about the future, even when you saw things more clearly than I did. Some of the best moments of my life have been by your side, especially our trip to New York, and I look forward to many more. I love you.
And I love you all!
