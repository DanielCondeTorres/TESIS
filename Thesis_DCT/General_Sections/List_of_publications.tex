This doctoral thesis has been written following the Compendium of Articles format and comprises four published articles and one unpublished manuscript divided in three chapters. Below, the list of publications included in this thesis is presented. For each article, full bibliographic information, co-authorship details, journal quality metrics, and a description of the author’s contributions are provided. Journal quality metrics correspond to the impact factor and quartile for the year of publication, as reported in the Journal Citation Reports (JCR), as provided by the Fundación Española para la Ciencia y la Tecnología (FECYT).

Chapter \ref{chap1} \textbf{Towards a Chirality-Aware Coarse-Grained Force Field} contains three articles focused on the development, validation, and application of chirality-aware coarse-grained force fields and computational tools for the simulation of cyclic peptides and their self-assembly in membrane environments:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Authors:} \textbf{Alfonso Cabezón}, Martín Calvelo, Juan Ramón Granja, Ángel Piñeiro, and Rebeca Garcia-Fandino.
    
    \textbf{Citation:} Cabezón, A., Calvelo, M., Granja, J. R., Piñeiro, Á. \& Garcia-Fandino, R. Uncovering the mechanisms of cyclic peptide self-assembly in membranes with the chirality-aware MA(R/S)TINI forcefield. Journal of Colloid and Interface Science 642, 84–99 (2023). doi: \href{https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jcis.2023.03.101}{10.1016/j.jcis.2023.03.101}.

    \textbf{Metrics:} In 2023, the Journal of Colloid and Interface Science was ranked Q1 in Chemistry by JCR, with an Impact Factor of 9.4.

    \textbf{Contributions:} As first author of the article, I contributed to the conceptualization of the project, conducted all the experiments presented, and participated in writing the results.

    \textbf{Citation:} Cabezón, A., Garcia-Fandino, R. \& Piñeiro, Á.
